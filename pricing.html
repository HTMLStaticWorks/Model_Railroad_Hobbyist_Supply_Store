Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, our monthly plans are month-to-month and can be canceled at any time. You won't be charged for the following month after cancellation.

Do discounts apply to sale items?

Membership discounts usually stack with regular sales unless otherwise stated. Some limited edition items may be excluded from additional discounts.

How do I access workshops?

Online workshops are accessible via our member portal. In-store workshops require reserving a spot through the workshop schedule page or by calling our store.
