DCC Installation & Programming

Upgrade your fleet with modern digital control.

What is DCC?

Digital Command Control (DCC) is a standard for a system to operate model railways digitally. When equipped with DCC, locomotives on the same electrical section of track can be independently controlled.

We provide professional installation of DCC decoders, including sound decoders, into almost any scale locomotive.

What We Offer

Decoder Install

Hard-wiring and plug-and-play installations.

Sound Upgrades

Adding high-quality speakers and sound files.

LED Lighting

Upgrading old bulbs to bright, efficient LEDs.

Programming

Custom speed curves and momentum settings.

Our Process

  • 1. Assessment: We evaluate the locomotive for space, motor health, and light paths.
  • 2. Selection: We recommend the best decoder brand (ESU, SoundTraxx, Digitrax) for your specific model.
  • 3. Installation: Careful hard-wiring or milling of the chassis if required.
  • 4. Testing: Rigorous track testing to ensure perfect operation.
