The Ultimate Scale Guide

Not sure which scale is right for your project? From tiny Z scale to large G scale garden railways, our comprehensive guide helps you choose the perfect size for your available space and interests.

  • HO Scale (1:87) - The most popular worldwide.
  • N Scale (1:160) - Perfect for smaller spaces.
  • O Scale (1:48) - Classic, larger size trains.
  • G Scale (1:22.5) - Great for outdoor garden railways.
