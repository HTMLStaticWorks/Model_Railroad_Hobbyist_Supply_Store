3D Interactive Anatomy

Anatomy of a Layout

A truly professional model railroad is built like a complex layered sandwich. Hover over the interactive 3D model stack on the right to see the exploded structural layers separate in space!

TOP
Scenic Cover (Top Layer) Hyper-realistic mountains, synthetic trees, ground ballasts, weathered scale buildings, and mini figures.
MID
Track System Network (Mid Layer) High-conductivity nickel-silver rails, sound-deadening cork roadbed, and automated turnouts.
BASE
Subgrid & Benchwork (Base Layer) Solid wood L-girder framing, custom birch plywood roadbed subgrids, and structural support pillars.
