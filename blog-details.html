Creating Realistic Water Features

By John Smith • October 15, 2026

Adding a water feature to your model railroad is one of the most effective ways to break up the landscape and add a level of realism that draws the viewer's eye. Whether it's a roaring river, a calm lake, or a small pond, the techniques remain largely the same.

1. Preparing the Base

Before you even think about pouring resin, the base must be absolutely sealed. Even a pinhole will allow expensive epoxy to drain away onto your floor. We recommend using a layer of plaster cloth covered by a thick coat of latex paint.

2. Painting the Depth

Water isn't just blue. It reflects the sky and the surrounding terrain. To create the illusion of depth, paint the center of your riverbed a dark blue-green or brown, and gradually lighten the color as you approach the banks. This simple trick adds tremendous realism once the clear resin is applied.
